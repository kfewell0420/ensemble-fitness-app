How it works

1

Prepare Your Track

Upload your original music in MP3, M4A or WAV format. Keep files under 10 MB where possible.

2

Tell Us About You

Add your artist name, track title, short bio and a link where members can discover more of your music.

Upload Your Music

Upload your track directly through our secure submission form. Review the terms and submit it for approval.

4

Get Heard!

Once approved, your track joins our Music Library, where members can discover it and add it to their workout videos.

License to use your music

By submitting a track, you grant Ensemble Fitness a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to feature your track inside member-uploaded workout videos on the Ensemble Fitness platform, and to use short clips of it in Ensemble Fitness's own marketing for the Music Library. You keep full ownership and copyright of your music — this is a license, not a transfer of rights.

Your rights to submit it

You confirm that you wrote or otherwise hold all necessary rights to every element of the track (including any samples, loops, or collaborators), and that using it as described above won't infringe anyone else's rights. You're responsible for your submission if that turns out not to be true.

Removal requests

You can ask us to remove your track from the Music Library at any time by emailing us — once removed, it stops being offered to members going forward. Because member videos that already used your track are their own published content, removing a track from the library does not automatically edit or take down videos that were already made with it before your removal request. If you have a specific concern about an existing video, contact us and we'll work with you on it directly.

Review & acceptance

Submitting is free. A real person reviews every track for fit before it's added, and Ensemble Fitness may decline or remove any submission at its discretion. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms on your behalf.
