Submit to the Music Library

Get your music into members' workout videos.

The Music Library is a curated set of real tracks from independent artists that members can pick for their workout videos instead of — or alongside — recording their own voiceover. It's free to submit, there's no review fee, and your name, bio, and link travel with every play.

Before you send anything: tracks should fit an active, motivational vibe — instrumental or clean vocals both work, but no explicit lyrics and no samples you don't hold the rights to. A real person on our team listens to every submission before it goes live.

Submitting is free — there's no review fee like there is for Ensemble Reads. By submitting, you're granting Ensemble Fitness a non-exclusive license to feature your track inside member-uploaded videos; you keep full ownership of your music and can ask us to remove your track at any time.

How it works

1

Prep your track

Export it as an MP3, M4A, or WAV file. Keep the file size reasonable (under ~10MB is ideal) so it loads quickly for members.

2

Write your artist info

Have these ready: track title, your artist name, a line or two of bio, and a link — Spotify, Instagram, your website, wherever you want people to find you.

3

Email it to us

Send the audio file and your info to partners@ensemblefitness.com with the subject line "Music Library Submission."

4

We add it — you're live

Once it's approved and added, your track shows up as a music option every time a member uploads a workout video, with your name and bio attached. We'll also email you a quick link to link a bank account through Stripe, so you're ready to receive tips directly the moment that feature launches.
