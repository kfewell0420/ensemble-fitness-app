Payouts

Get paid directly

Link a bank account through Stripe so book sales or music tips can pay out straight to you — Ensemble Fitness never sees your bank details, only Stripe does.

  1. Click "Link Your Bank Account" below.
  2. You'll be taken straight to Stripe's secure page — verify your identity and add your bank details there. Takes a few minutes.
  3. You're brought right back here, done. Payouts happen automatically after that.

Important: always start by clicking the button below. Don't go create a Stripe account on your own anywhere else — only the button below connects to Ensemble Fitness.
