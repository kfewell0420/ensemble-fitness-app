How it works

Five steps to your next workout partner.

From opening the app to finding a relevant partner should feel like minutes, not paperwork — because getting there together, not alone, is the whole point.

1

Create your profile

Just your date of birth, ZIP code, and the time of day you naturally like to train — no selfie check, no 15-step questionnaire.

2

Find members near you

Search by ZIP code to see who's around, and filter by wake-up time — early bird, night owl, or anywhere in between.

3

Browse real profiles

Check out each member's bio along with their photos and videos, so you know who you're looking at before you ever show up.

4

Match your schedule

Wake-up time tags make it easy to spot people whose natural training hours line up with yours.

5

Make the connection

Found a good match? Many members list the best way to reach them right in their bio, so you can connect and set up your first workout together.

Trust & safety

Built to be safer than a stranger DM.

Real, verified people — and privacy controls that put you in charge of what others can see. Helping one another stay safe is part of what "together" means here.

Verified members

Identity checks and a selfie/liveness scan help keep fake accounts out.

Your location stays private

We never expose your exact home address — just an approximate distance or general area.

Block & report, everywhere

Every profile, check-in, and conversation has one-tap block and report controls.

You control visibility

Choose who sees your check-in: everyone nearby, verified users only, matches, or partners only.

Meet in public, active places

We encourage first meetups at staffed gyms, parks, courts, and studios — never a private address.

Active moderation

Ongoing review for harassment, explicit content, impersonation, and unsafe behavior.

Ensemble Fitness does not use exact GPS tracking or promise to verify the age of every uploaded photo. We label recent, in-app-captured photos and combine verification methods rather than overselling what technology can prove.
