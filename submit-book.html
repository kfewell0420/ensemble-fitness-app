Submit to Ensemble Reads

Get your short book in front of our community.

We publish short-form (30 pages or fewer), on-brand motivational books from independent authors. You keep 75% of every sale — we don't take a cut of your rights, just a share of what actually sells.

How it works

1

Prepare your manuscript

A PDF, 30 pages or fewer, plus an optional cover image (JPG or PNG). Have your blurb and suggested price ready too.

2

Fill out the form below

Your title, name, contact info, blurb, price, and files. You'll continue to a small review fee at checkout.

3

We review it for quality & fit

Every submission is reviewed by a real person. We look for overall quality, readability, obvious spelling or formatting issues, proper page layout, and content that aligns with the positive, inspirational spirit of Ensemble Fitness.

If something minor needs attention, we'll let you know so you have an opportunity to correct it before approval.

4

Approved books go live

Your book appears on Ensemble Reads, and you keep 75% of every sale. We'll send you a secure link to connect your bank account through Stripe so payouts can be made automatically.

Ensemble Fitness never sees or stores your banking information.

Before you submit: Books should be short-form (30 pages or fewer) and centered on fitness, health, personal growth, encouragement, or motivation. Every submission is personally reviewed to help maintain the quality and positive spirit of Ensemble Reads.

Our review is designed to protect your work, not change your voice. If we notice an obvious typo, formatting issue, or something that needs clarification, we'll give you an opportunity to correct it before a final decision.

A small review fee is collected at checkout to cover the review process. If your book isn't accepted, your review fee is automatically refunded.
We'll send review updates and payout info here.
