Our philosophy

Member trust comes first.

A marketplace only works when you can trust what's being recommended. That's why we're selective about the products we feature — if we wouldn't recommend it to our own family and friends, we won't recommend it to you.

We look at quality, value, customer experience, and real-world feedback — and we keep listening after a product is listed. If a product falls to 3 stars or below, it comes off our marketplace.

Your trust matters more than an advertisement.

The Ensemble Fitness Trust Standard

We don't just sell everything.

Fit Marketplace holds products to a quality standard — protecting members and reputable brands, without punishing a product over a handful of reviews.

1

We listen to our members

Your experience matters.

Verified ratings and member feedback help us identify products that may not be delivering the quality, value, or experience we expect.

2

We review before we react

A rating tells us where to look. People help us understand why.

When a product is flagged, the Ensemble Fitness team reviews the feedback and overall pattern before making a decision.

3

If it doesn't meet the standard, it doesn't stay

Trust comes before advertising.

Products that consistently fall to 3 stars or below and no longer meet our standards are removed from the Fit Marketplace.

Advertisers can purchase exposure. They cannot purchase our trust.
