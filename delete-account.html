Delete Your Ensemble Fitness Account

How to request deletion of your account and your data

This page applies to the Ensemble Fitness app and the Ensemble Fitness member site (app.ensemblefitness.com), provided by Ensemble Fitness.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@ensemblefitness.com from the email address on your account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account, and include your name as it appears on your profile.
  3. We will reply to confirm it is you, and we will delete your account within 30 days of confirming.

What we delete

  • Your profile, including your name, email, bio, and profile photos.
  • The photos and videos you posted, your reactions, and your comments.
  • Your check-ins and location information.
  • Your notifications and your followers and following lists.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Safety and legal records, such as reports about an account or content, may be kept for up to 12 months so we can investigate abuse and meet legal obligations.
  • Routine backups are overwritten within 35 days of deletion.
Questions? Contact support@ensemblefitness.com.
