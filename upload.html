Payouts

Link your bank account to upload music

So tips from members can pay out straight to you, every song in the Music Library needs a linked bank account first — this only takes a few minutes, and Ensemble Fitness never sees your bank details, only Stripe does.

Before you start, have these ready:
  • Your full legal name and date of birth, matching a government-issued ID
  • Your home address (a P.O. box won't work)
  • Your Social Security Number
  • Your bank account and routing numbers

Stripe may also ask for a quick photo of your driver's license or passport to confirm your identity — that's normal.
